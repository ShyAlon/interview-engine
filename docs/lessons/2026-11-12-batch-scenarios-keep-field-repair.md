field independently. Never replace derived identity/provenance from model
output. Preserve valid fields when repairing a peer, and keep depth/provenance
checks after assembly. Journal validated item progress instead of every field
start/end; raw console diagnostics can stay detailed without a transaction each.

claiming savings. Fewer calls does not prove proportional time or credit savings.
